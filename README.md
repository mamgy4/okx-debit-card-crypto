## Why is my debit card payment declined?

A failed payment does not always mean that OKX is unavailable. Common causes include:

### The card issuer blocks cryptocurrency transactions

Some banks automatically decline digital-asset purchases. Contact the bank if the transaction is repeatedly rejected, but do not repeatedly submit the same payment without checking whether the issuer has placed a security block.

### Billing information does not match

The billing name and address entered for the payment should match the card issuer’s records. Small differences in postal code formatting can sometimes cause verification to fail.

### The card is not supported

OKX’s instructions refer to Visa and Mastercard, but acceptance still depends on region, card type, payment provider, and local rules. A prepaid, virtual, business, or foreign-issued card may not be accepted even when another card from the same network works.

### You reached a payment limit

The purchase provider may impose minimum or maximum transaction limits. Your bank may also impose daily spending limits.

### Identity verification is incomplete

Some purchases cannot proceed until account verification is completed. This is especially common when the card, fiat currency, or transaction size triggers additional checks.

### The payment provider is temporarily unavailable

A provider can reject or pause transactions even when the exchange itself is operating normally. Trying another supported payment method may resolve the issue, but the displayed purchase quote should be checked again because prices and fees can change.

## How to reduce the total cost

You do not need to overcomplicate a small purchase, but a few checks can prevent unnecessary costs.

1. **Compare the final received amount, not only the advertised fee.**
   A lower headline fee can still be offset by a wider conversion spread.

2. **Check the quote before confirming.**
   Review the fiat amount, crypto amount, payment fee, and expiration time.

3. **Compare card purchase with cash balance.**
   OKX says purchases funded from the cash balance can be completed without a purchase fee, although the original deposit route may have separate conditions.

4. **Avoid unnecessary currency conversion.**
   If your card is denominated in USD but you purchase in EUR, your bank or payment provider may apply an exchange-rate markup.

5. **Use a limit order when price control matters.**
   A debit-card purchase is convenient, but a spot limit order may give you more control over the execution price. It also introduces the normal spot trading fee structure.

6. **Do not chase VIP status for a small purchase.**
   The current VIP thresholds are far above the needs of most casual buyers. The practical question is usually whether the card quote is acceptable, not whether you can reach a high trading tier.
